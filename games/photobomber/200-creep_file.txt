CREEPS
================================================================================================
creep rules are read from top to bottom. if rule can't be achieved, it is skipped.
================================================================================================
CR1 = Social behavior
  1 = seeks groups
  5 = neutral
  9 = avoids groups
CR2 = Fear of light
  1 = seeks bright areas
  5 = neutral
  9 = seeks dark areas
CR3 = Danger response
  1 = curious / approaches danger
  5 = neutral
  9 = flees danger
CR4 = Navigation style
  1 = very predictable
  5 = average
  9 = highly erratic
CR5 = Energy expenditure
  1 = conserves energy
  5 = average
  9 = burns energy aggressively
